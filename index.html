CPG Ads Manager

CPG ad campaigns on DoorDash were fully manual — advertisers had to go through a rep, and each campaign took 5–8 hours to set up internally. I led 0-to-1 design of DoorDash's first self-serve Ad Manager, defining the vision and core flows for creation, targeting, and reporting. The platform launched with select CPG partners and became the foundation for a $171M business with 500+ active advertisers. It cut time-to-onboard by 80% and removed per-campaign overhead from internal teams.
