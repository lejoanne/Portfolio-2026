Multi store shopping

What seemed like a simple customer pain point—having to shop across multiple storefronts for the same retailer—was actually a much larger platform problem. I helped bring teams together around a shared vision that grew into a funded initiative with 14–15 engineers, led design across multiple workstreams, and partnered closely with Product on strategy and execution. The work is now helping unlock DoorDash's expansion into new categories and superstore experiences, with the first launches rolling out in mid-2026.

Checkout redesign

I led a complete overhaul of the customer checkout experience across restaurant delivery and retail/grocery orders, simplifying a checkout flow that had become cluttered and confusing over time. I worked closely with teams across payments, subscriptions, loyalty, growth, and engineering to launch a more streamlined and scalable experience that improved customer confidence while setting us up for future expansion.

The redesign drove $144.4M in projected annual incremental GOV, improved checkout conversion by 3.8%, and brought roughly 123K more people back to ordering on DoorDash each month.

— Selected Work / 2022—2026

Universal Savings Interface

Deals were scattered across ads, banners, loyalty programs, and merchant promotions, making them easy to miss and difficult to compare. I led the rollout of a unified savings experience that gave customers a single place to discover offers while standardizing promotions across previously disconnected teams and systems. The experience scaled across major surfaces, increased DashPass signups by 2.9%, and reduced operational overhead by $225K annually.

— Selected Work / 2022—2026

CPG Ads Manager

Building and managing CPG ad campaigns on DoorDash was fully manual — advertisers had to contact a rep, and each campaign took 5–8 hours of internal setup. It didn't scale. I led the 0-to-1 design of DoorDash's first self-serve Ad Manager, defining the vision and core flows for campaign creation, targeting, and reporting. The platform launched with a select set of CPG partners and became the foundation for what's now a $171M business with 500+ active advertisers, cutting time-to-onboard by 80% and freeing internal teams from per-campaign overhead.
